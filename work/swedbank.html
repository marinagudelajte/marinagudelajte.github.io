Swedbank Investment School

An online course that teaches the basics of investing to anyone in the Baltics.

  • E‑learning
  • Web
  • Mobile
  • 3 languages

Together with the INTEA team, we created the course's visual experience: a landing page, 10 learning modules, 100+ videos and email communication, adapted for web, mobile and three languages.

Investing is a topic many people find complicated or intimidating. The course had to be understandable for a broad audience with different levels of experience, work equally well on desktop and mobile, and stay consistent across three countries and languages, all within Swedbank's strict brand guidelines.

The brand book left little room for visual experimentation, so I focused on what makes a complex topic easy to learn: a modular system of layouts, components and illustration rules. I designed a set of screen types (theory, infographics, exercises, quizzes) and rebuilt each one for mobile instead of simply scaling it down.
