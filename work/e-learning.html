E‑learning design

Course templates that clients buy at a fixed price and fill with their own topic.

  • Templates
  • Soft skills
  • Hard skills
  • Fixed price

These are templates for course builders, made for soft skills and hard skills courses. A client buys a template at a fixed price, the content is then written from scratch for the topic of their course, and the visuals stay as designed.

For the client, this means a fully designed course with a clear budget from the start, since nothing has to be drawn from zero.

For me, it meant designing every screen to work with any subject: layouts have to hold shorter and longer texts, and the visuals have to suit topics nobody has written yet.
